INTERNAL POLICY — Remote Work Guidelines v2.3
Effective: 1 January 2026

Eligibility: Permanent staff with 6+ months service.
Remote days: Maximum 3 per week. Core hours 9am-3pm EAT.
Equipment: Laptop provided. Internet allowance KES 3,000/month.
Security: VPN mandatory. No company data on personal devices.
Violations result in immediate return to office.
